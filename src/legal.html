Nocturne has zero tolerance for content involving minors, coercion, or non-consent. You must not upload, request, link to, or promote:

What a notice must include

  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed.
  • The exact URL or location of the allegedly infringing material.
  • Your name, address, telephone number, and email address.
  • A statement of good-faith belief that the use is not authorised.
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

We may forward notices to the uploader and may remove or disable access to the material. Counter-notices must meet the requirements of 17 U.S.C. § 512(g). Accounts that repeatedly infringe may be terminated. Misrepresentations in a notice may create liability under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f).

Creators, verification & consent

Nocturne is built for verified, consenting adult creators. Publishing requires the following, without exception:

  • Age verification: government-issued photo ID plus a liveness check, reviewed before publishing.
  • Performer verification: every person appearing in content must be independently verified as 18 or older and must consent in writing.
  • Consent records: signed, dated consent for each scene and collaborator, describing the content and the scope of release.
  • Ongoing consent: any performer may revoke consent. We remove affected content and may suspend the account pending review.
  • Record-keeping: records are retained as required by applicable law and are available to authorised inspectors.

Creators must be able to demonstrate that they own or control the rights to everything they upload. Content that is coerced, secretly recorded, impersonates a real person, or involves anyone under 18 is prohibited and will be reported.
