HOW TO PLAY

01

MOVE

W A S D or the left stick. Drag anywhere to orbit the camera. Shift to sprint, Space to jump.

02

DRIVE

Walk close to any car and press F or tap JACK. The left stick controls throttle and steering.

03

FIGHT

Click or use the fire button. R reloads. Gunfire draws attention, and attention draws stars.

04

GET PAID

Follow the gold marker, recover three encrypted drives, then reach the pink dead drop before the timer ends.
